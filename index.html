Profile

A grounding in trial work, argued through to appeal

Abid R. Shaikh holds degrees in Law (LL.B.), Criminal Law (LL.M.), and English Literature (B.A.), and practices before the Constitutional and Trial Courts. Matters are carried from the first hearing through appellate litigation, with the same brief handled end to end rather than passed along at each stage.

That combination — courtroom experience plus a background trained in close reading and argument — shapes how each case is prepared: thoroughly researched, and put in terms a client can actually follow.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Any documents relevant to your matter — prior court orders, FIR copies, property deeds, contracts, or notices received. Arranging them in chronological order helps in assessing your case efficiently.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Clients based outside Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar can consult by WhatsApp video call or Google Meet. A prior appointment and consultation fee apply.

How are legal fees determined?

Fees depend on the complexity of the matter, the court involved, and the time the matter is likely to require. This is discussed transparently at your initial consultation.
